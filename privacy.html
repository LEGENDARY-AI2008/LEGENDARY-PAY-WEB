Fingerprint login and payment approval happen on your phone. Your fingerprint never leaves your phone and Legendary Pay never receives or stores it. We store only a public key your phone creates, which cannot be used to recover your fingerprint.

  • Flutterwave: payments, bank transfers, bill payments, airtime, virtual accounts, and identity checks.
  • OpenRouter: the AI service that powers Lega. It receives only the questions you ask Lega and the balance and recent-transaction details described above.
  • Render (server hosting) and Vercel (web app hosting): they store and process data for us. Their servers may be outside Nigeria, including in the United States. We rely on their data processing terms for protection. [confirm DPA links]
  • Our email provider: to send account emails such as codes and receipts.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with others only as needed for the services above, or when the law requires it.

6. Your rights

Under the NDPA 2023 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information, subject to the records we must keep by law;
  • restrict or object to some uses of your information;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

You can download a copy of your data at any time in the app: Me → Settings → Download my data.

To use these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We will respond within the time the NDPA requires. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

7. Deleting your account

Open the app, go to Me → Settings → Delete account, and confirm with your PIN or fingerprint. Your wallet must be at ₦0 first. When you delete your account we erase your name, email, phone number, PIN, device details, saved people and bank details, reminders, schedules, notifications, and the fingerprint keys stored for your phone. We keep your transaction and payout records, and the hash of your ID number, for the periods in section 5. After deletion you cannot sign in again with the same phone number or email.

8. Keeping your account safe

Every payment needs your PIN or fingerprint. We limit repeated wrong PIN attempts. Keep your PIN private. Do not share codes sent to your email with anyone, including people who say they work for us. We will never ask for your PIN by phone, email or chat.

9. Security and breaches

We protect your information with access controls, limited staff roles, and an audit log of staff actions. If a breach puts your personal data at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and we will tell affected users without undue delay.

10. Who can use Legendary Pay

Legendary Pay is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
